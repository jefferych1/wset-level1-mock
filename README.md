# WSET Level 1 Wines – Unofficial Mock Exam

A free practice exam for the WSET Level 1 Award in Wines.

- 30 multiple-choice questions per attempt, drawn at random from a bank of 151
- Same weighting as the real paper: 6 / 18 / 6 across the three learning outcomes
- Optional 45-minute timer, pass mark 70% (21/30)
- Review of every question you got wrong, with the correct answer and an explanation

**Play it:** https://jefferych1.github.io/wset-level1-mock/

Unofficial study aid. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the Wine & Spirit Education Trust (WSET). Questions are original, written against the published Level 1 Award in Wines specification.
